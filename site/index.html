Why professionals choose PROCURATOR

Other agents ask you to trust the model. This one doesn't need you to.

The approval layer lives in a broker the model cannot route around. The model drafts; you approve the exact draft; the broker executes once. That single design choice is what makes it safe to give an agent your inbox, your phone line and your logins.

Approvals bound to exact content

A send, call, submit, purchase, build or post is approved with a code tied to a hash of what will happen. Change a recipient, an amount or a file and the approval is void. Replays are no-ops. Timeouts never retry blindly.

Hosted agents describe "permissions"; here the check is cryptographic and outside the model.

Your own server, your own keys

One small Linux box you control. Credentials stay in 0600 files the model never reads; passwords are typed by you on a private page, never by the assistant. Nothing is used to train anyone's model.

Consumer agents run on the vendor's cloud computer and some train on your data by default.

Real phone calls, with you listening in

It calls from its own number, works through automated menus, waits on hold and talks to the representative. Listen-in mode calls you first so you can merge in silently and take over at any moment; it keeps transcribing after you do.

No Pixel required, no call-center humans behind the curtain, no telemarketing-grade scripts.

Signed-in web errands that can't double-submit

Cloud browser or Chrome on your home PC. The broker classifies every click; final buttons need your review; a final is never pressed twice unless the page proves the first press failed. Banks are never browsed. CAPTCHAs are yours.

Most agents stop at public pages; the ones that log in rely on the model to decide what is "consequential".

Long projects as task packets

Describe a multi-week goal in Markdown — or let the Packet Composer build it with you. The orchestrator works in sessions inside your waking hours, asks questions in chat, stops at every approval, records evidence, and proposes "done" only with proof. Budgets per packet.

Always-on agents keep working; PROCURATOR keeps working and keeps receipts.

Code built on your machines, as draft PRs

Ask for a change; approve the exact build; your Mac or Windows PC runs Claude Code or Codex on your own subscription and opens a draft pull request. Nothing merges or deploys by itself. The server never runs commands on your computers.

Unique among personal agents.

Health logistics under consent

Optional module for adults: appointments, portal reads, refills and nutrition logging run under grants you approve with exact limits. "Requested" is never reported as "booked". Health details never reach memory, posts or anyone outside the granted institution.

Logistics, not medical advice. A clinician and emergency notice is always shown.

Any model, visible cost

Switch between Claude and GPT models in one tap, per chat, per packet, per task. A daily budget warns at 80% and pauses background work at 100%. Routine browser steps run on a cheap model; decisions run on a strong one.

Subscriptions hide the meter. PROCURATOR shows it.

Approvals, by design

Six classes of action. Only one of them runs without asking.

Every tool declares its class in a manifest. The broker enforces the class; the model can neither lower it nor invent a tool the manifest does not list.

read

Looks things up and runs freely: calendars, inbox search, page snapshots, quotes, evidence search.

draft

Prepares something for you to approve. Nothing leaves: an email draft, a call brief, a post preview.

own_write

Changes your own things, no third party involved: a task, a reminder, an event in your own calendar.

owner

Sends you a review or acts on owner-only state. The review carries the exact line you approve with.

external

Reaches a third party — only after your hash-bound approval: a browser submission, a portal booking.

public

Publishes to an audience after /postsend; account, text and media are all bound into the approval.

What it never does, whatever it is told

These are hard blocks in code, not instructions in a prompt. Page text, emails, documents and callers are treated as data, never as instructions.

  • Browse banks, brokerages or payment apps
  • Type or store passwords, one-time codes, card or Social Security numbers
  • Solve CAPTCHAs
  • Press a final button twice, or press any final without your approval
  • Move money outside your own store accounts and your cap
  • Permanently delete email, sign documents, or merge and deploy code
  • Disable a smoke, CO or leak detector (home module)

Two editions, one codebase

PROCURATOR for professionals. PROCURATOR‑X for the ones who also ship code.

Same brokers, same approvals, same installer, same version number. The developer edition adds a coding orchestrator that turns a task packet into a draft pull request on your own machine. On a standard box those tools do not exist — not hidden, absent from the policy — so the assistant is never tempted to half‑use them.

PROCURATOR

  • Task packets for multi‑week projects, with the Packet Composer

Developer edition

PROCURATOR‑X DEVELOPER PREVIEW

Everything in PROCURATOR, plus a remote coding orchestrator for complex, multi‑step engineering tasks.

  • Everything in the standard edition
  • Code builds: a packet becomes a draft pull request on your Mac or PC, with Claude Code or Codex on your own subscription
  • Builder relay: your machines pull work over a restricted key; the server never runs commands on them
  • Composer "coding" packets, /buildapprove with the exact repo, branch and goal bound in the hash
  • Builds tab on the dashboard; nothing is merged or deployed by itself
  • Planned: a box‑side coding sandbox with its own container and volume, walled off from mail and account secrets

sudo bash install.sh --repo … --domain … --edition x

Switch later by re‑running the installer with the other edition; your state, accounts and packets stay. The edition is a one‑line flag the brokers and the plugin gate both read (EDITION / PROCURATOR_EDITION).

Install

Paste one line. Open one link. Tap Start.

No terminal skills, no domain, no pairing codes. The installer sets up everything on your server, turns on HTTPS, and hands you a one-time link; the rest happens in your browser and on your phone. About twenty minutes, most of it waiting.

1

Rent a server, paste one line

Any Ubuntu 24.04 server (a $24/month 4 GB droplet). Paste the command into its console: Docker, the pinned gateway, the brokers and automatic HTTPS — on your domain, or a free one it makes for you.

2

Open your setup link

Paste your AI key and the token BotFather gives you. Then tap Open my bot on your phone and press Start — that's the pairing. From then on, only you can talk to it.

3

Connect Apple and Google

The link lands you on Accounts, with step-by-step help: one email and one app password lights up Mail, Calendar and Contacts; Google sign-in adds Calendar, Contacts, Tasks and Drive.

Ongoing research

PROCURATOR is a research instrument as much as a product

PHI is a non-profit studying how autonomous agents can take administrative load off clinicians and other professionals without taking decisions away from them. The code is the experiment; the batteries are the measurements.

  • Approval-bound agencyCan an agent be useful when every consequential action is bound to a human's exact approval? We measure completion, approvals per task and cost per task across live sites, phone menus and portals.
  • Consent-scoped health logisticsGrants with explicit limits, one effect per operation, "requested" kept distinct from "confirmed", and data labels enforced at every outbound path. Synthetic EHR and pharmacy services make the whole flow testable.
  • Autonomous web browsing benchmarksGraded batteries over consumer sites on cloud and home browsers, offline fixtures from real failures, and a regression set with a fixed judge.
  • Small models for clinical documentationCompanion work on local, small-model documentation assistants and the verification needed before a note is trusted.
  • Primary-care workflow optimisationMulti-arm studies of AI-augmented primary care workflows, from pre-visit preparation to follow-up.
